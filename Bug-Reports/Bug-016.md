**Title**: Delivery Fee Is Waived for Orders Below the Free Delivery Threshold Due to an Expired Pilot Offer

**Severity:** Medium  

### Description
The system applies free delivery to an order with a seller net amount below the configured free delivery threshold, even though the displayed promotional offer has already expired.
The Operator Settings specify a free delivery threshold of ₹1,000 and a delivery fee of ₹100 before tax. However, the Buyer Checkout page displays ₹0 delivery fee for an order with a net amount of ₹720.
The Buyer page also displays the promotional message:
"Pilot OPS-14: free delivery on all your orders until 31 Oct 2026."
The current date is 17 November 2026, which is after the offer expiry date.

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the Buyer Portal.
2. Add products from a seller with a combined net amount of ₹720.
3. Navigate to the Checkout page.
4. Review the delivery fee and promotional offer displayed in the order summary.

### Actual Result
- Seller net amount: ₹720
- Delivery fee: ₹0
- The expired Pilot OPS-14 free delivery offer is still displayed and appears to be applied.

### Expected Result
Since the seller net amount is below the configured ₹1,000 free delivery threshold and the promotional offer expired on 31 October 2026, the system should charge:

- Delivery fee: ₹100
- Delivery fee GST (18%): ₹18
- Total delivery charge: ₹118
