**Title**: Buyers Can Place Orders for Quantities Exceeding Available Stock

**Severity:** High  

### Description
The system allows buyers to successfully place orders even when the combined ordered quantity exceeds the product's available stock.

### Test Data
- SKU: SKU-0339
- Product: Chef's Packaged Water 1L
- Case Size: 24 pieces
- Available Stock: 430 pieces

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in as Buyer 1 and order 8 cases (192 pieces).
2. Log in as Buyer 2 and order 7 cases (168 pieces).
3. Log in as Buyer 3 and order 3 cases (72 pieces).
4. Complete checkout for all three buyers.
5. Verify the total ordered quantity against the initial available stock.

### Actual Result
The system successfully accepts all three orders, resulting in a combined order quantity of 432 pieces, even though only 430 pieces were available.

### Expected Result
The system should validate the cumulative ordered quantity against available stock and reject any order that would cause the total ordered quantity to exceed the available inventory.
