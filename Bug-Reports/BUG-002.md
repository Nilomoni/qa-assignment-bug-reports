**Summary:** 
Product quantity shows a negative value in Seller's My Products section after a buyer places an order

**Severity** - High

**Description:**

When a buyer places an order for 2 cases of Pure Basmati Rice, the corresponding product quantity in the Seller's My Products section displays a negative value instead of reflecting the correct stock deduction.

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Log in as a Buyer.
2. Add 2 cases of Pure Basmati Rice to the cart.
3. Place the order successfully.
4. Log in as the Seller.
5. Navigate to My Products.
6. Check the available quantity of Pure Basmati Rice.

**Actual Result:**
The product quantity displays a negative value in the Seller's My Products section after the order is placed.

**Expected Result:**
The product quantity should be correctly updated by deducting the ordered quantity (2 cases) from the available stock. It should not display an incorrect negative value.
