**Enhancement** - [Buyers – Cart] Provide an option to remove products from the cart before checkout**


**Description:**
Currently, buyers do not have an option to remove an already added product from the cart before proceeding to checkout.

An option to delete or remove individual products should be available on the Cart page so that buyers can modify their cart before placing an order.

**Expected Behaviour:**
- Each product in the cart should have a Remove/Delete option.
- Buyers should be able to remove individual products from the cart before checkout.
