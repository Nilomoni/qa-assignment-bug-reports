**Title:** [Buyers – Cart] Insufficient spacing between Quantity field and Update button

Severity - Low

**Description:**
The Quantity field and Update button on the Cart page are displayed too close to each other, with no proper spacing between them. This affects the visual alignment and overall user experience.

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Log in as a Buyer.
2. Add a product to the cart.
3. Navigate to the Cart page.
4. Observe the spacing between the Quantity field and Update button.

**Expected Result:**
Adequate spacing should be maintained between the Quantity field and Update button for a clean and properly aligned layout.

**Actual Result:**
There is no visible spacing between the Quantity field and Update button.
