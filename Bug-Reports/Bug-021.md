**TItle**: Seller GSTIN and Delivery Fee Are Missing from Generated Tax Invoice

**Severity:** Medium   
**Related Requirement:** BR-19

### Description
The generated tax invoice does not display the seller's GSTIN or a separate delivery fee line item. Both fields are required as per the invoice generation requirements.

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the Buyer Portal.
2. Open a delivered order.
3. Navigate to its tax invoice.
4. Review the seller details and invoice line items.

### Actual Result
- Seller name (WholeCart Direct) is displayed, but Seller GSTIN is missing.
- No separate delivery fee line item is displayed.

### Expected Result
- The seller's GSTIN should be displayed along with the seller's name.
- A separate delivery fee line should be displayed, including ₹0 when no delivery fee is charged.

### Evidence
Invoice: INV-00030  
Order: WC-01022-1  
Invoice Date: 2026-11-17

Invoice: INV-00017  
Order: WC-01013-3  
Invoice Date: 2026-11-17
