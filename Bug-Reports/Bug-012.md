**Title**: Incorrect Refund Amount Displayed When Refunding Remaining Quantity After Partial Refund

**Severity:** High  

### Description
When a buyer requests and receives approval for a partial refund, the system incorrectly calculates the refund amount for the remaining quantity during a subsequent refund request.
After successfully refunding 1 piece out of 2, the buyer attempts to refund the remaining 1 piece. Instead of displaying the correct refund amount of ₹640.50, the system displays an incorrect amount of ₹12.81.

### Preconditions
- A buyer has placed an order for 2 pieces of any product.
- Unit price: ₹610.00
- Tax rate: 5%
- Original order total: ₹1,281.00

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the Buyer Portal.
2. Place an order for 2 pieces of product.
3. Complete the order delivery.
4. Request a partial refund for 1 piece.
5. Log in as the Seller and approve the partial refund.
6. Verify that the buyer receives a refund of ₹640.50.
7. Return to the same order as the Buyer.
8. Select the remaining 1 piece for a second refund request.
9. Check the displayed refund amount.

### Actual Result
The system displays an incorrect refund amount of ₹12.81 for the remaining 1 piece.

### Expected Result
The system should calculate the refund amount correctly, including the applicable 5% GST.

- Product amount: ₹610.00
- GST refund (5%): ₹30.50
- Expected refund amount: **₹640.50**

The total refunded quantity across all approved refund requests must not exceed the originally ordered quantity.
