**Summary**: Next Button Remains Active on the Last Page of Seller Catalogue

**Severity:** Low  

### Description
On the Seller Catalogue screen, when navigating through the product pages using the Next button, the Next button remains active even after reaching the last page where no further products are available.

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the application as a Seller.
2. Navigate to the Catalogue screen.
3. Click the Next button repeatedly until reaching the last page containing products.
4. Observe the Next button.

### Actual Result
The Next button remains active and clickable even on the last page, where no additional products are available.

### Expected Result
Once the last page containing products is reached, the Next button should become disabled or inactive and should not allow further navigation.
