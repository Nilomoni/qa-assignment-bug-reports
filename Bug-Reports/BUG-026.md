**Title:** [Invoices] Invoice does not display delivery fee, tax breakdown, and total amount as per the design

Severity: Medium

**Description:**
As per the expected invoice format, the generated tax invoice should display product details and delivery fee as separate line items, followed by the final invoice total.

However, the generated invoice displays only the product details and does not show the delivery fee, tax breakdown, or final total amount.

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Log in as a Buyer.
2. Place an order containing a product with a delivery fee.
3. Navigate to the Order History page.
4. Open the generated tax invoice.
5. Verify the invoice line items and total amount.

**Expected Result:**
The invoice should display product details and delivery fee as separate line items, for example:

- Item Name (case) × 2 … 5% … ₹2,318.40
- Delivery fee … 18% … ₹118.00

The invoice should also display the final total amount, including the applicable taxes and delivery fee.

**Actual Result:**
The invoice displays the product details but does not show the delivery fee as a separate line item. The tax breakdown and final total amount are not readable.
