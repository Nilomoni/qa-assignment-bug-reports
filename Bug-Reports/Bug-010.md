**Title**: Out-of-Stock Products Are Still Visible in Buyer Catalogue and Search Results


**Severity:** Medium  

### Description
Products with zero available stock are still displayed in the Buyer's catalogue or search results, contrary to the defined inventory business rules.

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in as a Seller and ensure a product has a stock quantity of 2 pieces.
2. Log in as Buyer A and place an order for all 2 available pieces.
3. Verify that the product stock becomes 0.
4. Log in as Buyer B.
5. Search for the same product in the catalogue.

### Actual Result
The product Test Taaz Tea (SKU-0308) remains visible in the Buyer's catalogue/search results despite having 0 available stock.

### Expected Result
Products with zero available stock should not be displayed in the Buyer's catalogue or search results.
