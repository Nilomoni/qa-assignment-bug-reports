**Summary** - Cart quantity field displays incorrect value when entering 100

**Severity** - Medium

**Description:**

When the user enters 100 in the Quantity field of the Cart page, the system displays 2 instead of automatically adjusting the quantity to 1.
As per the validation message, the allowed quantity must be a whole number between 1 and 99. Therefore, entering a value greater than 99 should set the quantity to the minimum allowed value of 1.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Navigate to the Cart page.
2. Enter 100 in the Quantity field.
3. Click the Update button.
4. Observe the updated quantity.

Actual Result:
The quantity changes to 2 instead of 1.

Expected Result:
The quantity should automatically be adjusted to 1 when an invalid value of 100 is entered, as per the expected minimum quantity requirement.
