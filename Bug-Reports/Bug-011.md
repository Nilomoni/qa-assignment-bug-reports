**Bug**: Buyers Cannot Increase or Decrease Product Quantity from the Cart Page

**Severity:** Medium 

### Description
The Buyer Cart page does not provide any option to increase or decrease the quantity of products already added to the cart.

As a result, buyers cannot modify the quantity directly from the cart page.

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the application as a Buyer.
2. Navigate to the Product Catalogue.
3. Add any product to the cart.
4. Open the Cart page.
5. Observe the product quantity field.

### Actual Result
There is no option or control available to increase or decrease the product quantity directly from the Cart page.

### Expected Result
The Cart page should provide a quantity dropdown for each product, allowing buyers to increase or decrease the quantity from 1 to 20.

Any quantity change should automatically update the corresponding line total and cart net total.
